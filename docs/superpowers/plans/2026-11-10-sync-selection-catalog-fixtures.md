## Global Constraints

- “No capture lanes or ongoing pages are required for either catalog admission case.” Both new factories require zero capture enrollment and expose no capture operation.
- “Subjects are returned only after genuine complete qualification inside the factory.” No SQL-manufactured import, cloned bundle, fake passing proof or A artifact relabeled as CSV.
- “All economic expected values remain independently authored literals.” Full record, metadata, receipt and origin checks stay in each scenario and its independent suite; no shared expected-result generator.
- “The function does not publish scopes/policies or write a selection file.” C0 consumes installed policy/selection; enrollment retains publication and its no-replace selection write. B consumes its author's existing files unchanged.
- “A must remain unapproved and unimported.” B's incidental A proof, registered scope and policy entry confer no selecting authority.
- “Individual nested subject-runner message lengths and timings are **unobserved**.” Measure only existing accessible outer boundaries; retain unchanged nested enforcement plus complete genuine reports as separate compliance evidence.
- “Missing/skipped/unavailable coverage, incomplete fixture inventories or failed cleanup means the prerequisite is not qualified.” Preserve every existing cap/deadline and exact comparison, including `bytes >= financialMessageLimit` rejection.
- “Normal close is idempotent and attempts every cleanup action even if one fails, reporting cleanup failure without masking the original construction error.” Own resources immediately and clean only the allocated namespace/root/listeners/pools.
- No production API, schema, DDL, compiler, protected producer, saved pin, fixture barrel or existing integration assertion changes. No mode flags, hooks, injected launcher, arbitrary SQL callback or public control/owner pool/root/reviewer handle.
- These are qualification and extraction tasks: no artificial RED, intentionally broken production code or financial fallback. Actual producer rejection remains an unqualified prerequisite and goes to its owner with a safe exact error.
