## Why the plan is implementable and aligned

1. **The additive schema accounts for runtime005's actual limitations.** Plan lines 38–57 preserve its nonempty monetary receipts and ongoing economic origins, retire only future application-role insertion into the inadequate old delivery association table, and introduce separate immutable unit/outcome history plus application heads. The scoped financial-record key can be added without changing runtime002. The initial origin tuple can FK to runtime002's existing scoped import key/receipt uniqueness; the ongoing reference can FK to runtime005's scoped receipt uniqueness. Additional composite unique keys needed for exact import/lane/inbox/outcome references are explicitly assigned to 006. The table responsibilities and constraints do not require destructive migration or a second monetary-receipt authority.

2. **Initial and ongoing origin domains converge on an enforced common uniqueness key.** Both existing economic tables receive insertion triggers that claim one common `record_id` row. This handles concurrent SQL claims across the domains, rather than merely trusting a union query. Typed original-receipt FKs, scoped record FKs, canonical validation, and target-record membership checks preserve F1/F3/F5 identity and original-receipt semantics. Backfill is restricted to validated concrete origins; missing or contradictory history fails instead of fabricating ownership or progress. Task 1 explicitly tests direct app-role claims and privilege restrictions as well as service reads.

3. **Receipt membership and economic ownership remain distinct.** Plan lines 51, 73–88, and Task 3 give the mixed R0/R1 example concrete expected IDs and cents. R1 acknowledges replay plus new events while replay resolves to R0 and only the new allocation belongs to R1. Coalesced deliveries share their producer's record/origin, and exact repeated deliveries do not acquire a second outcome. All-replay bookkeeping has a separate response and immutable unit, preserves multiple typed originals, and cannot add a monetary receipt, target version, snapshot, or fictitious lineage. This directly repairs PR39's F5 violation.

4. **The captured subject is complete within the declared narrow bound.** `readCoverageCut` has a single-statement interface and explicit bounded lane/checkpoint/position/inbox/page results, independent anti-orphan checks, source-wide guards, exact canonical membership, and historical outcomes/origins through F. The 1,000-event limit covers all retained post-baseline history. The input is the entire suffix F→C, and any semantic history, foreign/unknown scope, incident, extra lane/stream, missing membership, or unresolved prefix rejects the attempt. Full canonical W/W′ equality binds heads and guard predicates; later exact-key reads may validate immutable content but cannot enlarge the cut. This implements the accepted observation-cut boundary without silently adding a capture-to-commit exclusion guarantee.

5. **Authority, locking, and recovery are separated correctly.** Plan lines 103, 111, and Task 3 require historical recovery before source preflight or current target/epoch freshness, while comparing the full canonical submitted plan or replay subject and saved committed identity. Original receipt/audit epochs remain unchanged even if the retry carries today's epoch. New effects and new all-replay bookkeeping require active current authority through commit. The existing `lockCurrentAuthority` supplies the scope/head fence; target, complete identity set, progress, and activity follow in the established order. Capture is never locked in reverse order. Every SQL retry reacquires service-owned tokens and repeats the authoritative reads; stale authority or changed coverage is not converted into an enlarged retry.
