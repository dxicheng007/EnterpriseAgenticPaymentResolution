## Finding

**P2 — State how nested runner size evidence is obtained, or narrow the measured-size claim.** Design lines 157 and 207 require actual producer/runner message lengths through call-through observation, and line 207 also requires recording each actual successful invocation. The available parent-process seams expose financial/bootstrap evaluator requests and results, but not their nested subject-runner messages. Financial evaluation occurs in `packages/evaluator/src/financial-attestation-evaluator.ts:94`; its parent launcher returns the validated completion at `packages/evaluator/src/financial-attestation-launcher.ts:43`. Bootstrap subject executions occur inside the separate evaluator at `packages/evaluator/src/bootstrap-evaluator-runner.ts:37`; the completion at line 60 contains cases/assertions and baseline results, not per-runner byte lengths. The transport starts separate Node processes with an empty environment (`packages/evaluator/src/transport.ts:33`), measures bytes privately, and returns only a parsed value/failure (`packages/evaluator/src/transport.ts:106`). Vitest spies installed in the qualification process do not propagate into those subprocesses.

A builder therefore cannot literally satisfy measured nested-runner lengths through the proposed existing-export observers without adding an unplanned instrumentation mechanism or claiming inferred sizes as observations. The narrow correction is to explicitly enumerate measurements available at existing outer call-through boundaries (actual evaluator request/response and launch-context framing, accessible evidence-pin/sidecar file lengths, total observed invocation time), and distinguish these from nested-runner limits established by unchanged transport enforcement plus successful complete mandatory reports. Record that individual nested-runner lengths/timings were not observed. Preserve every existing cap/deadline and all full financial/bootstrap requirements. If individual nested message telemetry is actually mandatory, identify and separately review a concrete feasible observation seam before release; do not silently modify protected producers or execute substitute proof runs. This is a qualification-evidence specification gap, not evidence that the proposed fixture exceeds a limit.
