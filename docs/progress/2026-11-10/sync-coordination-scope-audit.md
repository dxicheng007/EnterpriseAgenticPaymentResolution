## Finding

P2 is already substantially specified: continue durable capture while a customer is held; discover exact typed prerequisites across captured streams; release only a revalidated complete financial group; let independent known scopes progress; preserve per-stream capture/application distinctions and visible unresolved gaps; catch up from the original approval vector; support complete append-only Company B batches with byte identity and batch continuity. No new monetary policy is needed for these behaviors.

The next work is not simply a poll loop around P1. P1 intentionally rejects all foreign/unknown/semantic input, extra lanes, historical incidents, multiple groups, and more than 1,000 lifetime post-baseline events. P2 needs a separately reviewed selection/coverage and persistence extension before those cases become executable. Keep P1 unchanged until accepted. Its complete-prefix evidence and original-receipt transaction are useful foundations, not a general scheduling API. Sources: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-10-sync-apply-repair.md:15–24,42–57,86–105,205–209`; `docs/progress/2026-11-10/team-handoff.md:19–23`.

The older atomic-apply design's “resolution authority” warning is narrower than a blanket policy blocker for P2. Frozen F1 already says a known-seller unknown account must not pause independently mapped accounts. Routing another enrolled customer's input to that customer's own grant is not a permission to discard it. A scheduler can keep source-wide unresolved coverage visible without claiming those positions financially applied or globally resolved. Any request to waive an unresolved financial/identity/semantic conflict remains outside the present authority and must be separately specified. See the exact distinction below.

## Exact remaining coordination work

1. **Bounded source scheduling:** Enumerate genuine committed lanes/streams; fetch outside runtime transactions; resume every stream from its own durable cursor; handle empty/refetch/replay accurately; retry transport failures with bounded backoff and durable restart behavior. Capture must continue independently of customer dependency holds and preserve visible pause behavior. One busy/failing lane must not starve others. No scheduler may infer permanent caught-up state from one empty page.
2. **Trusted routing and coverage:** Resolve known enrolled scopes from committed profiles and preserve raw unassigned holds. Detect source-wide versus selected-customer blockers across the authoritative captured cut, including semantic history and other lanes. Avoid declaring a globally unknown account merely because one profile rejected it. Record enough immutable routing/coverage evidence to audit exclusions without fabricating financial outcomes.
3. **Durable pending/quarantine state:** Persist exact event/group identity, reason, typed dependency keys, examination identity/count and bounded next work trigger. Repeated process polls or crash retries must not consume a fresh examination or append duplicate activity. Define safe claim/lease/cancellation and whole-transaction retry behavior as technical design choices. Keep historical attempts immutable and current state recoverable.
4. **Dependency closure/release:** For the earliest blocked selected scope, find exact already captured providers across streams, bound the whole closure, preserve capacity/reversal coupling, reclassify against authoritative target, and commit only a fully valid group. Later prerequisite input may bypass the hold for this purpose; unrelated later same-customer work may not bypass the ordering hold. Missing, ambiguous, held, cyclical or over-limit providers remain explicit work, with no funds consumed.
5. **Application selection expansion:** Replace P1's blanket rejection of independent-scope and semantic coverage with reviewed, mechanically verified selected-unit coverage. Replan separate complete groups against the actual target version each will commit; never slice a combined planner plan/lineage. Remove the artificial lifetime 1,000 limit only with bounded durable coverage/history proof, not `LIMIT 1000` plus an unsupported claim of completeness. Retain global identity/origin checks and current authority through commit.
6. **Contiguous progress and gaps:** Persist per-event results even when prerequisite order differs from source order; advance each relevant frontier only through proven outcomes. Keep source-global unresolved positions visible even while one account progresses. Distinguish a scan/inspection cursor from an applied frontier. A filled capture gap can be evidenced by actual contiguous immutable capture; historical incident retention is not evidence that the gap still exists, and disappearance from the latest-incident summary is not proof of resolution. Conflicting identity or governing-change incidents must never be cleared by elapsed time or later successful traffic.
7. **Company B capture:** Verify complete original batch bytes, descriptor membership, both event and batch sequence, and baseline originals before atomically moving either capture cursor. Batch size may exceed an application unit; transport capture completeness and financial 1,000-event closure are different bounds. Verify API receipt and CSV inputs together using the manifest vector; a page of decoded file events alone is not complete CSV capture proof.
8. **Safe operations reads:** Expose authoritative captured/applied/pending/quarantined coverage, separate batch/event gaps, receipts, as-of versions and honest unknowns. No summing incompatible monetary kinds into an apparent balance, no zero amount for unmapped data, and no zero lag without measured source high water. Experience's read/UI work consumes a released contract; P2 does not take its paths. `team-handoff.md:23,43,49–50`; `platform-sync-design.md:138–154`; apply repair `:57`.

## Genuine policy gaps versus owner decisions

**No new monetary policy found for the above path.** Identity, permitted financial kinds, allocations, reversals, replay, active authority, dependency release, unknown-account hold, independent progression and drift containment are already decided.

**Do not escalate these routine design choices as product questions:** table/DTO names; source/account scan representation; stable scheduling/fairness; exact finite retry/backoff settings; attempt deduplication; claim ownership; bounded query indexes; versioned audit extension; original-byte transport packaging; precise safe counts with unknown coverage. Existing constants are candidates for operational review, not authority to implement an unreviewed scheduler. The owners should freeze concrete shapes, examples, transitions and consumer changes.

**Narrow, genuinely absent authority:** no located contract grants an operator or worker a generic permission to mark unresolved financial/unknown-identity/governing-change work successfully resolved, discard it, clear a material conflict, treat new semantics as approved, or restate already imported money. If a proposed progress design requires any such action, the exact action, actor, evidence and effect on source-wide coverage require user clarification. Do not introduce it to make a frontier move.

**Important correction to the older open-question formulation:** F1 already allows independent mapped accounts to continue past another account's unresolved raw input. This can be represented as scoped routing/coverage without globally resolving the input. A validated unchanged semantic event is an observation, not monetary application or approval of a changed revision (`classification design:65,124–138`). A design may separately track observed nonfinancial coverage while retaining the financial frontier/gaps; naming such an observation is not itself a new financial permission. The exact public meaning of every frontier must be reviewed, but P2 need not be blocked awaiting a generic skip/resolve policy. The old `atomic-apply design:144` explicitly instructs the owner to locate existing authority before asking; frozen F1 supplies a key part of it.

**Remain out of scope:** reenrollment/version switching, automatic historical restatement, manual arbitrary skip/resolve, clearing sticky changed terms, new financial transaction types, new enrollment or generated-account grants, live provider setup, and worker AI. Containment and deterministic prerequisite revalidation do not require these additions.
