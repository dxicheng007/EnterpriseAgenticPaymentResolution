## Findings

- Exact proof selection is implementable without adding browser authority or a registry enumeration API. The private selection shape matches the actual A2 policy selection, including financial report ID and bootstrap fixture reference. Requiring one matching indivisible installed policy tuple, exact current registered scope/profile/manifest, full validated capture, and the real financial reader prevents treating references, public eligible summaries, or self-consistent hashes as issuance. Control must retain only safe references; protected descriptor validation remains in accepted setup/launcher code.
- S3 ports match the handoff. `reserve` creates a real job; `execute` returns no result; the persisted results table associates a unique result with that job. The registered reader validates completed provenance, reserved request, candidate, result and observed plan before returning `{result,plan}`. An adapter lookup by real job ID followed by this reader is sufficient. The pre-reservation expected binding can be constructed from already validated members using the actual frozen binding preimage; its job-independent nature does not justify a fabricated evaluator envelope. The actual reserved-input check and complete canonical proposed/observed-plan comparison preserve job and target provenance.
- The selected-result key includes policy tuple, full capture, recorded and planner target identities, version and full plan digest. Immutable winning selection and strict reread avoid proof-ID churn under identical preparation or races. Actual recorded parent bytes/time/hash are preserved separately from the cutover planning copy, as required by A3b/v2. Full capture is passed to planning so unresolved source-wide blockers cannot disappear through profile filtering.
- Semantic freshness and policy handling are consistent. Each preparation and new approval independently observes the accepted producer; selected exact revisions, missing keys and governing `*` retain frozen semantics. Only observation time is excluded from fingerprint, while original audit observations remain immutable. Loading installed policy once per process with restart rotation matches A2. Missing policy/selection disables new preparation/intents without falsely breaking read-only services or inventing eligibility.
- Null-aware captured tokens plus a unique head sentinel serialize absent and present heads. Comparing the captured token before fingerprint reuse prevents stale I/O from laundering itself as an identical review. Package/companion/head writes are atomic; the same head lock linearizes intent creation. External reads, subprocess work and publication remain outside that transaction. Exact key retries retain original canonical request, reviewer/session/time and bundle; authorization against immutable intent scope/source prevents outages or head corrections from erasing legitimate recovery or becoming a cross-scope lookup capability.
- The committed-intent reader matches the actual A2 publisher port. Publication after commit, immutable canonical bytes, and exact restart recovery are concrete. Republish/recover of an old pending intent is not a current-policy bypass: A4 returns only pending and performs no runtime application. A5 must still check current policy and independent current semantics for any not-yet-successful application. Conversely an already successful exact receipt must be recovered before current policy/head/semantic prerequisites; A4 has no authoritative receipt and appropriately defers that behavior to A5 rather than inventing success.

## Prepare and pending transport decisions

The additive authenticated `POST /api/review/:accountId/prepare` is consistent with the frozen zero-write GET requirement and protected proof execution. It makes the necessary mutation explicit, preserves existing route names and strict ReviewPackage/ActivationRequest shapes, and applies reviewer mutation/CSRF authorization. Persisted `GET /api/review/:accountId` is correctly described as a saved review rather than a fresh readiness guarantee. No frozen requirement makes preparation a GET side effect.

HTTP202 `{state:'pending',reviewPackageId,idempotencyKey}` before A5 is a consistent bounded transport result for a committed control intent, including publication retry. It is not a ReceiptResponse, does not claim `replayed:true`, and does not redefine the frozen successful `activateCustomer`/worker receipt contract. The later A5 integration must add authoritative receipt-first recovery and success behavior. Nothing in the reviewed handoff authorizes substituting pending control state for that success contract. The explicit partial-slice boundary and required tests make this additive transport decision reviewable without a new financial/product decision.
